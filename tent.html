Tent

November 10, 2025

Abstract & Background

WIP I went on a week long + camping trip with my best bud earlier this year. I have a couple different tents (one I got off Amazon in high school for $12, one my mom got from her work's points catolog, and one a friend gave me). When camping out in Shenandoah, I brought the Coleman my mom gave me - it is nice because it is big and can hold its own, but it's pretty clunky. It doesn't pack down easily and is really heavy. After admiring someone with a tent that looked to be extremely light weight, I knew I just had to make my own tent.

Method & Results

This is definitely one of the more daunting projects I've signed myself up for. There are so many moving parts (none of which I really know how to do). It's like going to the library, opening your computer, and staring at a blank google doc having no clue what you're about to write a ten page essay on. That surely is a feeling I never want to endure again. Anyhoo, like most projects, I go hard for a while, reach a road block, put it down for several months, then finally get back after it. My initial plan was to make a 2 person tent, similar to your standard REI tent. I essentially want to base my design (mainly dimensions) on theirs as much as possible. This [LINK] REI site proved to give me a good starting point. Then I did some light research to id other tent widths and lengths and heights till I settled on what I thought would be best to optimize weight, comfort, and material usage. Fig 1 is what my final design looks like.

Next, the real road block, was deciding on the poles. I went back and forth for two months on if I should make my own poles or buy them. Buying them would be cheaper, faster, and better, so I really wanted to do that, but deep down, I suppose I knew I had to make them from scratch. I even toyed with buying some used poles from Facebook Marketplace, but I couldn't find any good ones. In the end, I to make them myself. It felt disingenuous to not, and mostly, the flex of making the tent entirely from scratch far outweights the short term damage.
