Abstract & Background

After creating STSW, I thought it would be fun to send out a weekly newsletter to send music recs and keep the people up to date on my life.

Method & Results

I send these out via email but what I like about these is that each newsletter is reflection of, as Charli xcx would say, "how i'm feeling now." Sometimes I put a lot of time into the newsletter and they're semi creative. Other times, it feels more fitting for them to be to the point and concise.
